## 1. Purpose
Give employees one clear path to report security events, and give the FSO a consistent way to
assess, report, and track them.

## 2. Principles
- When in doubt, report it to the FSO. The FSO decides what goes to the government.
- Report promptly. Do not investigate on your own.
- Do not put classified details in unclassified email, chat, or tickets.

## 4. FSO workflow
1. **Receive** - log the report: [ticket ID, date, reporter, category]. Keep details need-to-know.
2. **Contain** - take immediate steps to protect information if needed.
3. **Assess** - decide whether the event is reportable under 117.8 and to whom.
4. **Report** - submit to the required government recipient in the required time and format.
5. **Inquire** - if required, conduct and document an administrative inquiry.
6. **Correct** - record root cause, corrective actions, owners, and due dates.
7. **Close** - document closure and any lessons learned for training.
