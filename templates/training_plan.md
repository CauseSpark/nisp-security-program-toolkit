# Security Training Plan - Template

> **Personal study material. Fictional company: Larkspur Systems, Inc.** Based on the public text
> of [32 CFR 117.12, Security training and briefings](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/part-117/section-117.12)
> and free public courses from [CDSE](https://www.cdse.edu/). Not an official plan. Course numbers
> change; confirm them in the CDSE catalog.

## 1. Goals
- Every cleared employee is briefed before first access and refreshed every year.
- Security staff are trained for their roles.
- Completion is recorded and overdue training is caught before it becomes a finding.

## 2. Training matrix
| Course ID (tracker) | Training | Audience | When | Rule ref | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| INIT-BRIEF | Initial security briefing | New cleared employees | Before first access | 117.12 | In person by FSO |
| ANNUAL-REFRESH | Annual security refresher | All cleared employees | Every 12 months | 117.12 | [LMS / in person] |
| INT101 | Insider threat awareness | All cleared employees | Initial, then annual | 117.12(g) | CDSE INT101.16 or equivalent |
| ITP-SPECIAL | Insider threat program personnel training | ITPSO and program staff | Once, on appointment | 117.12(g) | CDSE INT333 curriculum or equivalent |
| DERIV-CLASS | Derivative classification | Derivative classifiers | Before classifying, then as required | 117.13 | CDSE course or equivalent |
| FSO-ROLE | FSO role training | FSO and security staff | On appointment | 117.12 | CDSE FSO curriculum |
| DEBRIEF | Security debriefing | Departing cleared employees | When access ends | 117.12 | In person by FSO |

## 3. Process
1. HR notifies the FSO of new hires, role changes, and departures.
2. FSO assigns training and records the due date in the tracker.
3. Employees complete training; the FSO records the completion date.
4. Each month the FSO reviews the tracker for items due in 30 days or overdue.
5. Overdue items go to the employee's manager; repeated misses go to the SMO.

## 4. Metrics
- Percent of cleared employees current on annual refresher.
- Percent current on insider threat awareness.
- Number of overdue items at month end.
