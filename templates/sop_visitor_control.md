## 1. Purpose
Make sure visitors get access only to what they are cleared for and need to know, and that uncleared
visitors never get access to classified information.

## 2. Scope
- Visit types covered: [classified visits, unclassified visits to cleared areas, vendors, tours].
- Locations covered: [list cleared locations].

## 3. Roles
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| FSO | Approves classified visits; confirms clearance and need-to-know |
| Host employee | Requests the visit; escorts the visitor; confirms departure |
| Front desk / reception | Checks ID, issues badge, keeps the log |

## 4. Procedure
### 4.1 Before the visit
- [ ] Host submits a visit request at least [N] business days ahead.
- [ ] For classified visits: FSO confirms the visitor's eligibility and the need-to-know with the
      visitor's FSO or government sponsor through the approved system.
- [ ] FSO confirms which areas and information are in scope.

### 4.2 Arrival
- [ ] Check government-issued photo ID against the approved visit request.
- [ ] Record the visit in the log: [fields: name, organization, host, time in, badge number].
- [ ] Issue a visitor badge that is visibly different from employee badges.

### 4.3 During the visit
- [ ] Host escorts uncleared visitors at all times in controlled areas.
- [ ] Classified discussions happen only in approved locations.
- [ ] [Rules for phones, cameras, and other electronic devices].

### 4.4 Departure
- [ ] Collect the badge and record time out.
- [ ] Host confirms nothing was removed that should not have been.

## 5. Exceptions and problems
- Unannounced visitor asking for classified access: deny access and contact the FSO.
- Lost visitor badge or escort lapse: report to the FSO the same day; FSO decides whether it is
  reportable under the [incident reporting SOP](sop_incident_reporting.md).

## 6. Records
- Visitor logs kept for [retention period].
- Approved classified visit requests kept for [retention period].
